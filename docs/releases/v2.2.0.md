## ⪩ Premiere Deck v2.2.0 ⪨

A tidier, more compact window with the same title bar, type and control sizes as the other apps of the family, and a Scale setting that enlarges the window and its text.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Scale** — a setting under Appearance with five steps, 100 %, 125 %, 150 %, 175 % and 200 %, on top of the Windows display scaling. The window and its text grow together and stay sharp; the change applies at once and is kept for the next start.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **Title bar** — the module tabs (Actions, Files) sit in the centre of the title bar; the app icon, name and version on the left; settings, minimize and close in one capsule on the right.
- **Type and density** — one type scale for every text of the window and slightly smaller, evenly sized controls, buttons, switches and rows, so more fits on screen without crowding.
